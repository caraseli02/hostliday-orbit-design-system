## Design Context

### Users

Travelers using an AI-powered travel coordination app. They browse destinations (Explore), plan trips (Compose), execute trips in real-time (Navigate), and handle disruptions (Recover). Context varies from relaxed browsing on a Sunday morning to stressed navigation in a foreign airport.

### Brand Personality

Warm, confident, understated. Three words: **warm, precise, quiet**.

- Warm red as hero color (gradient #DA0000 → #810000)
- Photography-led, golden-hour, low-saturation imagery
- Sentence case everywhere, no emoji, no hype
- Verbs over nouns on action surfaces
- Orbit speaks in first person only on Navigate/Recover surfaces

### Aesthetic Direction

**Four distinct registers** — each surface has its own personality:

| Surface | Register | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| Explore | Warm, evocative, place-led | Light, photography-dominant |
| Compose | Pragmatic, consultative | Light, structured |
| Navigate | Terse, time-stamped, useful | Dark, map-first |
| Recover | Direct, accountable, human | Light/neutral, urgency signals |

**Anti-references:**
- NOT generic travel booking (Booking, Expedia)
- NOT AI-generated / template-like
- NOT cold corporate dashboard

**Typography:** Montserrat (display), Inter (body), JetBrains Mono (monospace/times)

**Animation:** Orbit-ease `cubic-bezier(.2, .7, .2, 1)`. Fade + translate entrances. No bounces/springs. Breathing pulse for live elements only.

### Design Principles

1. **Warm red as hero** — gradient is the signature, use sparingly
2. **Photography-led** — warm, golden-hour, full-bleed, no rounded corners on photos
3. **Quiet competence** — no hype, no emoji, verbs over nouns
4. **One dominant column** — defer to map, photo, or timeline
5. **Surface-appropriate voice** — each register has distinct tone and visual treatment
